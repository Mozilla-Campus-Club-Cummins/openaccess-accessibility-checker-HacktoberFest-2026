Manual review checklist

What automation cannot confirm

Tab-stop detection observes focusable elements and keyboard navigation in the tested browser state. It cannot reliably judge whether focus order is meaningful, focus indicators are adequate, every interaction works correctly, or dynamic states are accessible. Results are limited to the tested page state and browser. Human keyboard testing is required; these checks do not establish WCAG conformance.

What this scanner checks

Page titles, declared language, image alternatives, form labels, button and link names, and headings. Review keyboard navigation, focus, reading order, content quality, and visual contrast manually. A page with zero findings still needs human review.
